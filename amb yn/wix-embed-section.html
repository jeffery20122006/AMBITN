Academic Events

Conferences, Workshops, Seminars & Academic Activities

AMBI Tamil Nadu organizes state-level conferences such as TN AMBICON, CME programmes, workshops, seminars, and postgraduate (PG) quizzes to promote academic learning and knowledge sharing.

The association also encourages academic excellence through awards and medals, including the Dr. Gopala Krishnan AMBI TN Medal and the Dr. Ebenezer Medal.
